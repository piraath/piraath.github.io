Pirasanth & Shankavie

OUR CEREMONY

A journey through the traditions and sacred rituals of our Tamil Hindu wedding.

Tamil Hindu Traditions

Two Souls. Two Families.
One Sacred Journey.

Our wedding will be celebrated according to Tamil Hindu traditions, through a series of sacred rituals symbolising love, commitment and the joining of two families.

Each tradition carries its own meaning. For those experiencing a Tamil Hindu wedding for the first time, we invite you to discover the significance behind the moments you will witness during our ceremony.

01

Welcoming the Groom

Maapillai Varaverpu & Aalathi

Our ceremony begins with Pirasanth's arrival, as he is welcomed by Shankavie's family. The traditional Aalathi is performed to bless him, ward off negative energies and welcome him as we begin this new chapter.

02

Opening Prayers

Ganesh Pooja & Pallikai

We begin with prayers to Lord Ganesh, seeking his blessings and the removal of obstacles from our path. The Pallikai ritual follows, symbolising prosperity, fertility and abundance in our future life together.

03

Arrival of the Bride

Manamahal Varugai

Shankavie makes her entrance accompanied by her family and joins Pirasanth at the Manavarai, the sacred wedding stage. Prayers to Shiva and Parvathi, followed by the Navagraha Pooja, seek harmony and blessings for our life together.

04

The Sacred Fire

Agni & Homam

The sacred fire, Agni, is lit as a divine witness to our union. Offerings are made into the fire as symbols of purity, devotion and the commitment we make to one another.

05

The Joining of Our Families

Kanyadhanam

Shankavie's parents symbolically entrust their daughter to Pirasanth, giving their blessing to our marriage. More than the union of two people, Kanyadhanam represents the coming together of our two families.

06

The Koorai & Thaali

Blessing of the Wedding Saree & Sacred Necklace

The Koorai, Shankavie's traditional wedding saree, and the Thaali, the sacred necklace representing our marriage, are blessed. Shankavie then changes into her Koorai before returning for the most significant moment of our ceremony.

07
The Heart of Our Ceremony

The Sacred Union

Thaali Kattu

To the sound of traditional music, Pirasanth ties the sacred Thaali around Shankavie's neck, marking our union as husband and wife. As the Thaali is tied, our families and loved ones shower us with flower petals and their blessings.

08

Seven Steps & Final Blessings

Sapthapathi · Ammi · Metti · Arundhati · Asirvatham

Together, we take the seven sacred steps, representing the promises we make to support, respect and walk alongside one another throughout our married life.

The Ammi, Metti and Arundhati rituals symbolise strength, faithfulness and stability in our marriage. Our ceremony concludes with Asirvatham, as we receive the blessings and good wishes of our families and loved ones.

And so, our journey as
husband and wife begins.
