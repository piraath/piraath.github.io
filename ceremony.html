OUR CEREMONY

A journey through the traditions and sacred rituals of our Tamil Hindu wedding.

Tamil Hindu Traditions

Two Souls. Two Families.
One Sacred Journey.

Our wedding will be celebrated according to Tamil Hindu traditions, through a series of sacred rituals symbolising love, commitment and the joining of two families.

Each tradition carries its own meaning. For those experiencing a Tamil Hindu wedding for the first time, discover the significance behind the moments you will witness during our ceremony.

01

Welcoming the Groom

Maapillai Varaverpu & Aalathi

The ceremony begins with the arrival of Pirasanth, who is welcomed by Shankavie's family. The traditional Aalathi is performed to bless the groom, ward off negative energies and welcome him as he begins this new chapter.

02

Opening Prayers

Ganesh Pooja & Pallikai

The ceremony begins with prayers to Lord Ganesh, seeking his blessings and the removal of obstacles from our path. The Pallikai ritual follows, symbolising prosperity, fertility and abundance in our future life together.
